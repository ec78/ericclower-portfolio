# Product Development and Launch Education

My career has included extensive work at the intersection of product development, education, customer support, and go-to-market. I have helped turn technical product capabilities into usable features, marketable add-ons, practical examples, and customer-facing learning resources.

---

## Representative work

At Aptech Systems, I worked across product development, training, support, and GTM for GAUSS, a statistical programming and data analysis platform. I planned, developed, launched, and promoted GAUSS add-on products while also building education resources to help users understand and adopt new capabilities.

This role required close collaboration with software developers, economists, business leadership, customers, and support teams. I helped clarify product requirements, test functionality, develop examples, prepare documentation, and create content that connected product value to real user needs.

---

## What this work included

- Planning and supporting add-on product launches from concept through customer introduction
- Translating customer needs and technical capabilities into product scope, positioning, and educational resources
- Creating tutorials, examples, documentation, blogs, and demos for new functionality
- Coordinating with development and business teams on requirements, release readiness, and customer communication
- Supporting GTM through product-focused content, webinars, announcements, and customer education
- Using support patterns and customer feedback to improve documentation, messaging, and product experience

---

## Why it matters

Product launches succeed when users understand what changed, why it matters, and how to apply it. Education, examples, and demos are not afterthoughts; they are part of how technical products become usable and valuable.
