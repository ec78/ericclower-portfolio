# Education Strategy, Learning Paths, and Content Roadmaps

I design learning programs by starting with evidence: customer questions, support patterns, survey feedback, product priorities, and organizational goals. This approach helps education work move beyond one-off content requests and toward a clearer system for deciding what to teach, for whom, and why.

---

## Representative work

At IMPLAN, I used customer ticket trends, internal survey feedback, and AI-assisted analysis to identify recurring knowledge gaps and recommend structured learning paths for internal employee roles and external customer segments.

I also developed a learning content roadmap aligned with the company's international customer growth initiative. The roadmap prioritized resources based on customer needs, support friction, audience segment, product complexity, and market expansion goals.

---

## What this work included

- Analyzing customer questions and internal survey responses to identify common learning gaps
- Grouping needs by internal employee roles and external customer verticals
- Using AI tools to accelerate pattern recognition, summarization, and draft learning-path recommendations
- Translating findings into content priorities, course structures, and resource planning
- Connecting education priorities to broader business goals, including international customer growth
- Helping internal teams understand where education can reduce friction and improve customer success

---

## Why it matters

The result is a more intentional education strategy: fewer isolated content requests, clearer audience priorities, and better alignment between customer needs, product complexity, and business direction.
