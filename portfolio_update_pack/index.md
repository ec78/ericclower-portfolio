# Eric Clower

## Product education, developer enablement, and AI learning strategy for complex technical products.

I help people understand and adopt technical software by connecting product capability, customer needs, education strategy, and practical examples. My work spans customer education, developer-facing demos, documentation systems, product launches, analytics training, AI-enabled workflows, and learning programs for internal and external audiences.

I currently lead Education Services at IMPLAN, where I manage education programs, technical content, LMS migration, employee AI enablement, API learning resources, and metrics for evaluating program impact. Earlier, at Aptech Systems, I worked across education, product development, customer support, and go-to-market for GAUSS, a statistical programming and data analysis platform used by researchers, analysts, and academic institutions worldwide.

My best work sits at the intersection of technical product understanding and human learning: making complex tools usable, teachable, measurable, and easier to adopt.

---

## Where I create the most value

- **Developer and API education:** Demos, examples, tutorials, and guidance that help technical users understand access patterns, data workflows, integrations, and product use cases.
- **AI learning and workflow enablement:** Practical programs that help teams use AI tools responsibly, improve productivity, and redesign everyday work around better workflows.
- **Product education and launch support:** Training, documentation, messaging, examples, and enablement resources that help new capabilities reach users with less friction.
- **Learning systems and measurement:** Content roadmaps, LMS structure, learning paths, KPI dashboards, and feedback loops that connect education investment to adoption and user success.
- **Technical content and documentation:** Searchable docs, code examples, tutorials, blogs, videos, and support resources that make advanced software easier to learn and apply.
