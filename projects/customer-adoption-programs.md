# Premium Support and Strategic Customer Onboarding

## Context

Customers get value from a complex product when they use it well, early, and consistently. Two programs at IMPLAN focus on that directly: Premium Support, which offers customers deeper engagement and support, and onboarding for strategic, high-ARR customers, where a smooth start shapes the rest of the relationship.

---

## My role and scope

| Area | My role |
|---|---|
| Premium Support program | **Own.** I own the program, including participation monitoring, engagement, utilization, and program processes. |
| Strategic customer onboarding | **Contribute, in partnership.** I work with Key Account Managers and Customer Success Managers to improve onboarding for strategic and high-ARR customers. |
| Account relationships, sales, renewals, and expansion | **Not in my scope.** These are owned by other teams. |

---

## Premium Support

I own IMPLAN's Premium Support program, an ARR-contributing customer program focused on deeper engagement and support. My work includes monitoring participation, identifying engagement gaps, improving utilization, and refining program processes to strengthen the customer experience.

### What I do

- **Monitor participation:** track how customers in the program are using the services available to them.
- **Identify engagement gaps:** find customers who are not getting full value from the program, and understand why.
- **Improve utilization:** help customers make use of the support and resources the program includes.
- **Refine program processes:** make the program more consistent and easier to run, for customers and for the team delivering it.

A paid program only delivers value when customers use it. Treating utilization as something to monitor and manage, rather than something to assume, keeps the program focused on customer outcomes.

---

## Strategic Customer Onboarding

Onboarding for strategic and high-ARR customers is a shared effort. Key Account Managers and Customer Success Managers own the customer relationships; I work with them to make onboarding smoother and faster.

### What I contribute

- **Reduce onboarding friction:** identify the points where new customers get stuck and address them.
- **Improve the implementation experience:** help customers move from purchase to productive use with fewer obstacles.
- **Improve time-to-value:** focus onboarding on what customers need to accomplish first.
- **Structure the onboarding process:** bring more consistency to how strategic customers are onboarded.
- **Coordinate customer-facing resources and support:** connect customers with the right training, documentation, and support at the right time.

---

## Approach

- Start from what the customer is trying to accomplish, not from a fixed curriculum.
- Use participation and account data to find gaps early. See [Customer and Program Analytics](customer-program-analytics.md).
- Treat onboarding and program engagement as ongoing processes that can be measured and improved.
- Stay clear about ownership: account teams own the relationship; my role is to strengthen the programs and resources that support it.

---

## Outcomes

- Meaningful improvement in time-to-value for strategic customer onboarding
- Stronger, more systematic monitoring of Premium Support engagement
- Better process consistency for program delivery and onboarding
- Closer alignment between Education Services, Customer Success, and Key Account Management
