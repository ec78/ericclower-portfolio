# GAUSS Data Analytics Blog

## Overview

I managed and wrote for the [GAUSS Data Analytics Blog](https://www.aptech.com/blog/), a hub for tutorials, applied use cases, product announcements, and technical education.

The blog played a dual role: it helped users learn GAUSS and it supported product marketing by introducing new capabilities through practical examples. I translated advanced modeling topics into accessible guides that supported customer success, reduced support demand, and highlighted product strengths.
