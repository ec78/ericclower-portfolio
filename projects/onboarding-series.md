# GAUSS Onboarding Video Series

## Overview

The GAUSS onboarding video series was developed to help new users build confidence with the software more quickly. The project combined instructional design, scripting, production coordination, technical review, and alignment with support and marketing goals.

This project is a supporting example of my ability to turn complex software workflows into structured, accessible learning resources.

---

## My Role

As project lead, I oversaw the production process from content design and scripting to coordination with graphic designers and education support contributors. I helped ensure a consistent instructional style, technical accuracy, and alignment with onboarding goals across the series.
