# Customer and Program Analytics

## Context

Decisions about support content, onboarding, and customer programs are better when they rest on evidence about how customers actually use the product and the programs around it. At IMPLAN, I use customer and account data, alongside program metrics, to decide where my team and our programs should focus.

---

## My role and scope

- **Build and run:** reports in Salesforce and through its API to identify customer and account patterns.
- **Created:** the Education Services metrics dashboard used to evaluate program performance.
- **Use the results to inform:** support content, customer engagement, onboarding, Education Services priorities, and program strategy.

This is customer and program analytics in service of support and enablement decisions. It is not sales, pipeline, or revenue-operations reporting.

---

## What I did

### Salesforce and API reporting

I use Salesforce and its API to identify customer patterns and account insights that inform support content, customer engagement, onboarding, and Education Services strategy.

Typical questions this work helps answer:

- Which customers are, or are not, engaging with the programs available to them?
- Where do patterns in customer activity point to onboarding or content gaps?
- Which customer groups need different support, resources, or outreach?
- Where should Education Services focus next?

### Education Services metrics and program KPIs

I created an Education Services metrics dashboard to evaluate program performance, communicate value, and guide decisions about priorities. It is designed to support questions such as:

- Which programs are reaching customers and internal teams?
- Which resources are most useful?
- Where are users still struggling?
- Which investments appear to support onboarding, adoption, or support efficiency?
- How should future programs and content be prioritized?

### Program monitoring

The same evidence-driven approach applies to the programs I own, including participation and engagement monitoring for [Premium Support](customer-adoption-programs.md), so gaps can be found and addressed early instead of discovered late.

---

## How the analysis is used

| Analysis | Informs |
|---|---|
| Customer and account patterns | Customer engagement and outreach; onboarding priorities |
| Program participation and engagement | Premium Support utilization; program process changes |
| Support and content needs | Support content priorities, including the [Support Site modernization](support-site-modernization.md) |
| Education Services metrics | Program strategy and team priorities |

---

## Outcomes

- Better visibility into customer and program engagement
- Data-informed priorities for support content, onboarding, and Education Services programs
- Stronger engagement monitoring for customer programs
- Clearer reporting on program performance
- A shared evidence base for conversations with Customer Success, Product, and leadership
