# GAUSS Machine Learning Library: Product Evaluation, Launch, and GTM

## Context

The [GAUSS Machine Learning Library](https://www.aptech.com/blog/announcing-the-gauss-machine-learning-library/) was a paid add-on that brought foundational machine learning models to GAUSS. Its audience was GAUSS users: economists, researchers, and analysts.

Product judgment mattered as much as engineering: the add-on needed the right scope, an interface that felt native to GAUSS, output that users could interpret, and a launch that showed customers why it was worth using.

## Product Evaluation

- **Scope:** evaluated the proposed product scope: which models and capabilities belonged in a foundational release.
- **Use cases:** defined and evaluated the use cases the product needed to support for GAUSS users.
- **Competitive research:** researched competing machine learning tools to understand what users would compare GAUSS against.
- **API and syntax:** reviewed API and syntax design for consistency with GAUSS conventions and ease of use.
- **Testing:** tested the product, evaluating functionality and UX.
- **Output interpretability:** reviewed the readability and interpretability of model output, so results would make sense to users without extra translation.

---

## Launch and GTM

The product launched. I supported the launch and oversaw customer-facing GTM outreach, including:

- Email sequences
- Website content
- Tutorials and supporting examples
- Technical blog content

Documentation, tutorials, and examples doubled as adoption resources: they showed customers how to apply the new models to the kinds of problems they already worked on.
